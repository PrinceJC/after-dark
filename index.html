Show us the hero in you

Just like Clark Kent becomes Superman after his day job, show us who you become once your shift is over. HighCall After Dark is a TikTok transition video contest: your video moves from your everyday VA self to your favorite superhero, villain, or fictional character.

Film your two-part video, upload it on this page, and you could win cash prizes sent through GCash.

Your video: two parts

Create one TikTok transition video with these two parts, in this order:

1. The Shift

Show yourself working at your desk while wearing your HighCall shirt.

No HighCall shirt? A plain white shirt or top is fine for this part.

⚡

2. Hero Time

Transform into your favorite superhero, villain, or fictional character.

Make it come alive with matching music, creative acting, or quick dance moves.

Costume rules

Any superhero, villain, or fictional character costume is allowed as long as it follows HighCall VA standards and respects the beliefs of fellow contractors.

Keep it confidential

Do not show any patient information or confidential client details while filming. Check your screen, documents, and background before you record.

How to join

  1. Film your two-part transition video following the requirements above.
  2. Submit it through the official contest submission page — the form at the bottom of this page.
  3. Submit by November 25, 2026, 11:59 PM PHT. Late entries will not be accepted.

You don’t need to post your video on TikTok or have a TikTok account. Upload the video file directly here.

Judging criteria

Judges score each entry out of 100% on four criteria.

  • Relevance to Theme and Message

    30%

    How well the video follows the two-part prompt and the “After Dark” theme, and delivers a clear, compelling message.

  • Creativity and Originality

    30%

    A unique costume, a fresh storytelling approach, and an original take on the concept.

  • Execution and Production Quality

    25%

    Smooth transitions, stable footage, good framing and lighting, and clean editing.

  • Audio and Visual Appeal

    15%

    Clear sound and voice, fitting music, and overall production value.

Prizes

All prizes are sent through GCash.

Special award: People’s Choice

  • TikTok People’s ChoicePHP 1,000
  • Facebook People’s ChoicePHP 1,000

After the event on November 27, 2026, HighCall VA will post all entries on its official TikTok and Facebook accounts. The entry with the most reactions on each platform wins that platform’s People’s Choice award.

TikTok and Facebook have separate winners, and each receives PHP 1,000. Voting closes on November 30, 2026, at 11:59 PM PHT, and the People’s Choice winners are announced on December 1, 2026. Only reactions on HighCall VA’s official posts are counted.

You can win twice

An entry can win both a main award (1st, 2nd, or 3rd place) and a People’s Choice award.

Reminders

  • Follow both parts of the format: the Shift, then the Hero Time
  • Keep costumes and content respectful and in line with HighCall VA standards.
  • Never show patient information or confidential client details.
  • Submit only through the official contest submission page before the deadline.
  • By joining, you agree that HighCall VA may post your entry on its official accounts.
  • Prizes are sent via GCash. Winners will be contacted privately for payout details.
